Joao Rulff, who completed his PhD at NYU with OSCUR in 2025 with the dissertation *Visual Analysis of Multimodal Sensor Data*, joined the School of Applied Mathematics (EMAp) of Fundação Getulio Vargas in Rio de Janeiro in June 2026.

As a researcher at NYU, he was a senior contributor to [Autark](/software/#autark), [UrbanTrace](/software/#urbantrace) and [UrbanClipAtlas](/software/#urbanclipatlas), and mentored the student teams behind them at NYU, Universidade Federal Fluminense and FGV. He continues to collaborate closely with the project.
