Congratulations to two OSCUR PhD students at UIC on their 2026 College of Engineering awards:

- **Gustavo Moreira** received the COE Graduate Student Award for Exceptional Research Promise, which recognizes PhD students who demonstrate outstanding research accomplishments and exceptional promise as researchers during their doctoral training.
- **Leonardo Ferreira** received the COE Graduate Student Award for Teaching Assistance Excellence, which recognizes outstanding teaching assistants.

Gustavo led [Urbanite](/software/#urbanite) and contributed to [Autark](/software/#autark); Leonardo led [VA-Blueprint](/software/#va-blueprint) and contributed to [Sidewalk Stewards](/software/#sidewalk-stewards).
